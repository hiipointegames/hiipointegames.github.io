Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

HiiPointe Games LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates 37 - The Math Puzzle, available as a mobile app and as a website at 37themathpuzzle.app (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate the App.

Required Username (Non-Personal)

A username (display name) is required to play. This username:

  • identifies players within the game
  • displays scores, leaderboards, and statistics
  • saves gameplay progress

Usernames are pseudonymous, are not required to include real names, and are not considered personal information. Players should not include personal information in their username.

Optional Account Linking (Email and Password)

Players may optionally create a linked account to save and transfer progress across devices. If you choose to link an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password

This information is used only to:

  • authenticate your account
  • restore progress
  • enable multi-device access
  • provide account recovery
  • send you occasional emails about the weekly $37 giveaway — for example that the entry rules have changed, or that your account is not yet eligible to be entered — each with a way to opt out

Providing this information is voluntary. The App can be fully used without creating an account, but progress may not be recoverable without one. Email addresses are considered personal information.

Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • device type and operating system
  • IP address
  • anonymous device or advertising identifiers
  • crash reports and diagnostics
  • general gameplay metrics

This data is used to operate, maintain, and improve the App.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party advertising, analytics, hosting, and authentication providers. On the web version at 37themathpuzzle.app, advertising is served through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

These services may collect device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and ad-interaction data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control third-party data practices.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info/choices. On mobile, you may also limit or disable personalized advertising through your device’s privacy or advertising settings; these controls are provided by your device’s operating system and do not require action within the App. See Google’s partner policy for how Google uses data.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide gameplay functionality
  • save player progress
  • enable optional account features
  • maintain performance and security
  • serve advertisements
  • comply with legal obligations

We do not sell or rent personal information.

4. Data Sharing

We may share information only with service providers that help operate the App (such as hosting, analytics, authentication, or advertising partners). These providers may process information solely to perform services on our behalf.

5. Data Retention

  • Gameplay and username data may be stored locally on your device or on our servers
  • Account information is retained while your account remains active

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time by contacting us directly via email at contactus@hiipointegames.com.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us below.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences. We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Because account linking requires an email address, children should not create accounts without parental permission. If we learn personal information has been collected improperly, we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information, but no system is completely secure.

9. International Processing

Information may be processed and stored in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after updates indicates acceptance.

Promotions and Prize Draws

We run a free weekly prize draw. Entry requires a saved account with an email address attached, and entrants must be 18 or older and resident in the United States. No purchase is necessary and no payment is ever required to enter or to win. The official rules, including the full eligibility and entry terms, are published at 37themathpuzzle.com.

In connection with the draw we use personal information as follows:

  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older and live in the United States. Ticking that box is what enters you into the draw, so we record that you confirmed it and the date you did. We do not ask for, or store, your date of birth or any proof of address.
  • Your email address is used to contact you if you win. We send at most two emails about any one prize. Separately, we may send you occasional emails about the giveaway — for example confirming that you have earned an entry that week, telling you that the entry rules have changed, or telling you that your account is not yet eligible — and about other prize draws or promotions we may run in future. We send at most one entry confirmation per week. Every one of those carries a way to opt out, and opting out never affects your ability to play, to be entered, or to be paid if you win. Account emails such as password resets are not affected by that choice.
  • Payment details you choose to give us. If you win, we ask you to reply with a Venmo or Zelle handle so the prize can be sent. We use it only to pay that prize, we do not store card or bank account numbers, and we delete the handle once payment has cleared and any period we are required to keep records for has passed.
  • Your username may be announced publicly if you win, including on our social channels and on our websites. We do not publish your real name, your email address or your payment details.

If you would prefer your username not to be announced, email us at contactus@hiipointegames.com and we will withhold it. You can still claim the prize.
