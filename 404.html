37, The Math Puzzle

37 is a free daily math puzzle: you roll seven dice and combine them with addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, parentheses and exponents so that your equation equals exactly 37. Play instantly in your browser — no download required.

Choose your mode. Easy is untimed practice with plain white dice. Challenge adds colored power dice — green for add/subtract, blue for multiply/divide, red for exponents — with unlimited rounds to learn the rules. The Daily Challenge gives every player the same seven dice each day: beat the clock in one sitting, keep your streak alive, and climb the global leaderboard.
